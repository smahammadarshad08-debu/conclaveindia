NATIONAL REGULATORY BENCHMARKS

Harmonised Guidelines
India 2021.

Official accessibility criteria under MoHUA, DEPwD, and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016.

HG-2021 § 4.3

Ramps & Landings

  • Max Gradient: 1:12 (8.33%) for short runs; 1:15 to 1:20 preferred for long runs.
  • Clear Width: Minimum 1200mm; 1500mm or 1800mm for two-way traffic.
  • Landings: 1500mm x 1500mm at top, bottom, and every 9m of run.
  • Handrails: Continuous dual handrails at 750mm and 900mm height with 300mm extensions.
HG-2021 § 4.1

Footpaths & Walkways

  • Clear Walking Zone: Minimum 1500mm width unobstructed (1800mm in busy commercial areas).
  • Vertical Clearance: 2200mm free of hanging tree branches, signboards, or fixtures.
  • Cross-Slope: Maximum 1:50 (2%) for drainage without throwing wheelchairs off balance.
  • Surface: Firm, stable, non-slip (SRV >= 36) in wet and dry conditions.
HG-2021 § 4.2

Kerb Ramps & Crossings

  • Kerb Ramp Slope: Maximum 1:10 (10%) with flared sides (1:10 slope).
  • Lip Transition: Flush with road (0mm) or maximum 15mm rounded bevel.
  • Tactile Warning: 300mm wide band of blister tactile tiles 300mm before roadway edge.
  • Audible Signals: Beeping sound synchronized with green pedestrian phase.
HG-2021 § 4.5

Tactile Ground Surface Indicators (TGSI)

  • Guiding / Directional: Flat-topped parallel elongated bars indicating travel direction.
  • Warning / Hazard: Flat-topped blister studs indicating road crossings, stairs, or platform drops.
  • Contrast: Minimum 30% luminance contrast against surrounding pavement.

ACCESSMAP · IC26-04-04

Access is a
route detail.

The challenge asks for an accessible route map, barrier-report workflow, route filters, confidence labels, and a demonstration on a small mapped area. Suggested ministry alignment: MoHUA / DEPwD.

WHAT WE RECORD
  • Ramps, steps, kerbs, and crossings (slopes, widths, lip heights)
  • Surface condition, vibration factors, and temporary obstructions
  • Tactile ground surface indicators (TGSI guiding and blister tiles)
  • Accessible entrances, elevators, and transit connections
  • Source, observation date, inspection audit, and photographic evidence
CONFIDENCE LABELS
High Confidence Field-checked by trained auditor with date, physical measurements, and evidence.
Medium Confidence Located user report or multiple consistent community reports, not formally audited.
Low / Unknown Missing tags, unverified reports, or inferred geometry. Never silently treated as accessible!
DATA RECORD SPECIFICATION

Each feature contains geometry, type, observed measurements (slope, width, kerb lip, surface), access status (passable / restricted / blocked / unknown), source, observed_at, updated_at, confidence, moderation state, and evidence notes. An edit date is not an inspection date.

GOVERNMENT & STANDARDS ALIGNMENT

Directly aligned with MoHUA’s Harmonised Guidelines for Universal Accessibility 2021, Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 Sections 40–46, and Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan (Accessible India Campaign).
